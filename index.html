At Bengal Tree Foundation, we believe a greener Bengal begins one certified fruit tree at a time — GPS-documented, science-backed, and planted in the places that matter most.

About our work Explore programmes

Bengal Tree Foundation's work begins with a commitment to planting in the most critical urban and peri-urban green spaces across Kolkata and North 24 Parganas — where canopy cover is thin, communities are large, and every tree planted becomes a measurable act of restoration.

By addressing the "whole of a place" — its ecology, neighbourhood infrastructure, community need, and corporate sustainability goals — BTF creates scalable CSR tree plantation solutions that are rigourously documented, fully Schedule VII compliant, and built to last twelve months and beyond.

Together for Bengal

Science, community, and corporate purpose — rooted in the same soil.

Our plantation programmes combine GPS-precision field science with local community partnerships and measurable corporate ESG outcomes. Everywhere we plant, BTF and its partners are devoted to trees that actually survive, grow, and give back.

This includes working with KMC and gram panchayats to secure plantation sites on public land, collaborating with local communities to ensure maintenance is community-owned, and partnering with corporate CSR teams to close their annual Schedule VII filings with full documentation.

Bengal Tree Foundation knows lasting green cover only succeeds when the community has skin in the game. That's why every tree we plant is adopted, tagged, documented — and never forgotten.
