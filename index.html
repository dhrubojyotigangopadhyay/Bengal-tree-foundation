Who We Are

Bengal's Dedicated Environmental NGO

Bengal Tree Foundation is a government-registered environmental NGO headquartered in Barasat, Kolkata, West Bengal. Established in 2024, the Foundation focuses exclusively on tree plantation, urban green-cover restoration, and bird conservation across Bengal — engaging communities, volunteers, and corporate CSR partners through science-backed plantation programmes.

"To plant and nurture trees across West Bengal through community participation, scientific monitoring, and corporate CSR partnerships — ensuring every sapling becomes a thriving, documented, and permanently protected tree that benefits both nature and people."

Statutory Basis — Schedule VII

Tree plantation and environmental sustainability programmes are explicitly covered under Schedule VII of the Companies Act, 2013.


Applicable item: (iv) "ensuring environmental sustainability, ecological balance, protection of flora and fauna, animal welfare, agroforestry, conservation of natural resources and maintaining quality of soil, air and water."


Fully compliant Schedule VII CSR expenditure.

Every tree receives a unique GPS coordinate, is photographed at planting, and enters our tracking database. You receive photo proof and coordinate data within 48 hours of planting. Monthly reports include all geo-tagged records for the full 500-tree batch. The QR add-on module additionally provides a live tree profile accessible by any stakeholder at any time.
If survival falls below 85% at the annual audit, Bengal Tree Foundation replaces all casualties at no additional cost. A reserve budget (~10–12% per tree) is built into every programme specifically for this replacement. This commitment is contractually binding and reflected in the MoU your legal team will review before fund transfer.
We plant 8 certified native species: Mango, Guava, Jamun, Amla, Jackfruit, Lemon, Bel, and Chikoo — all minimum 7–8 ft nursery-raised. Fruit trees are chosen over timber because they provide food security, community livelihood from fruit yield, richer biodiversity, and superior carbon sequestration while delivering the same environmental CSR impact. They also create a stronger narrative for your stakeholders and annual reports.
